07 Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

How quickly can Paul come out?

Paul is available Monday through Thursday 9am–5pm and Fridays until 1pm. Within those hours, most calls get a same-day or next-day appointment. If you're dealing with an active leak or no hot water, call (707) 336-2290 directly — he'll work to fit you in as fast as possible. Paul is not available on weekends.

What brands do you service?

Paul services all major brands including Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, State, American, GE, Navien, Rinnai, Noritz, and more. If your brand isn't listed, just ask — chances are Paul has worked on it.

Should I repair or replace my water heater?

If your unit is under 8–10 years old and the repair costs less than half a new unit, repair usually makes sense. If it's older or has repeated problems, replacement is often more cost-effective. Paul will give you an honest assessment — no pressure either way.

How much does a replacement cost?

Costs vary by unit type (tank vs. tankless), size, and brand. Paul provides upfront pricing before any work begins — no surprise charges. Call (707) 336-2290 for a free estimate, or use the booking form above.

Is Medina's licensed and insured?

Yes, absolutely. Medina's Water Heater Specialists is fully licensed and insured, carrying all required certifications to perform water heater installations and repairs in California.

What areas do you serve?

Paul serves Suisun City, Fairfield, Vacaville, Benicia, Vallejo, Dixon, and surrounding Solano County. Not sure if you're in the service zone? Give us a call and we'll let you know right away.

Is a tankless water heater worth it?

For many households, yes. Tankless units provide endless hot water on demand and can cut energy costs by 20–30%. They also last longer — 20+ years vs. 10–12 for standard tanks. Paul can help you evaluate whether it makes financial sense for your home.
